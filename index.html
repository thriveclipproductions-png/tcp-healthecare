Q1

What exactly do I get in the Free Practice Audit?

+

A 30-minute call where we review your hospital's current online presence, ad spend, and patient acquisition funnel — then send a written diagnostic with 3 specific fixes you can action whether you work with us or not.

Q2

We've worked with agencies before — they didn't move the needle. Why is TCP different?

+

We don't sell 'content packages.' We're hired to move patient numbers. Every patient story, every ad, every procedure video maps to a measurable outcome — appointments, OPD footfall, or cost-per-patient — agreed before we shoot a frame.

Q3

Do you understand patient privacy and local healthcare regulations?

+

Absolutely. We shoot with signed patient consent forms, anonymize cases when needed, and follow NDHM and local clinical establishment guidelines. We also provide NDA coverage for proprietary procedures and patient data.

Q4

How long until we see results from the campaigns?

+

Brand-recall lift in 30 days. Cost-per-appointment reduction by week 6. Quality patient leads in the funnel by month 2 — assuming a typical mid-quarter launch. We share a weekly dashboard so you're never guessing.

Q5

What does a typical engagement cost?

+

Engagements start at the equivalent of one extra OPD day per month. We scope per-hospital after the audit — pricing depends on shoot days, ad spend, and campaign length. No retainer lock-ins.
